Thesis Week 1 — Scoping Bimanual Manipulation

Initial framing of the honours research problem and literature starting points

Focus this week

Clarifying the scope of the bimanual manipulation thesis and identifying the most relevant prior work.

Key questions

  • What specific dual-arm tasks are realistic on the available platform?
  • Which coordination strategies are practical to implement and evaluate within the project timeline?
  • How should success be measured?

Notes

Early reading has highlighted the gap between laboratory demonstrations of dual-arm systems and robust, repeatable behaviour under realistic uncertainty. The next step is to narrow the task set and define a minimal viable experimental setup.

Next

  • Platform capability inventory
  • Draft experimental scenarios
  • Continue literature survey
