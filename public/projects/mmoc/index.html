CSIRO MMOC

Internship supporting future Australian space activities at the Mission Management Operations Centre

Role: Intern2024–2025Status: Completed
SpaceSystemsInternshipCSIRO

Overview

Internship at CSIRO’s Mission Management Operations Centre (MMOC), contributing to systems and software that support future Australian space activities.

Background

The MMOC provides operational and technical support capabilities relevant to space missions. The internship placed me in a professional research and engineering environment focused on reliable systems and clear documentation.

Work & Contributions

  • Supported systems and software activities within the centre
  • Worked with professional engineering practices, documentation standards, and collaboration processes
  • Gained exposure to the operational considerations of space-related ground systems

Technologies & Environment

  • Professional research / government laboratory setting
  • Systems engineering practices
  • Collaborative software and documentation workflows

Outcomes & Lessons

The placement reinforced the importance of rigorous process, clear communication, and designing systems that remain reliable under real operational constraints. It also provided concrete insight into the Australian space technology landscape.

Future Direction

Experience from MMOC continues to inform my interest in space systems, reliable infrastructure, and the intersection of robotics and operational technology.
